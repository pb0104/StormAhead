One thin link to the engine

The console now loads real buildings from the server, but the Run button still replays 12 closures and 6 cut-off areas picked by hand from one engine run.

Real so far

Map, roads, flood outline, elevation, 7,115 buildings, 328 cell sites, the regional internet signal and 9 power-outage areas from night lights. Most resources, zones and evidence numbers are still mock.

No LLM anywhere

Every "agent" today is a deterministic rule or a small browser model. The engine labels its events "rule-based" and never presents them as AI reasoning.

Nothing is saved

Assigning a team or issuing an alert changes the screen only. No action is stored, so the system can't learn from what the analyst did.
